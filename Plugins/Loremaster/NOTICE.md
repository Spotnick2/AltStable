**Questie**, which are GPLv3:

- QuestieDB: <https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB>. Data by Muehe, TheCrux (BreakBB),

QuestieDB's licence notes that some historic content was contributed before any licence and
is "all rights reserved" where in doubt; the Questie team allows copying under GPLv3. The
Questie name and logo are not covered by any licence and are not used here.

`QuestData.lua` is generated by `Tools/QuestData/gen-quests.lua` (in the repository, not
shipped), which records the QuestieDB version and build commit it was made from in the file's
header. Zone names and continents come from the WoW Forever client's own tables (wago.tools).
